HAFYANI GLOBAL COLLABORATION

Connecting Artificial Intelligence, Industry and Global Opportunity

An open international initiative founded in Milan, Italy.

Our mission is to use responsible artificial intelligence to connect verified business opportunities, public procurement tenders, manufacturers, suppliers and qualified partners across international markets.

Our Vision

We believe that valuable opportunities should be accessible to businesses of all sizes, regardless of geography, language or access to information.

Open Invitation to Collaborate

We welcome independent developers, AI researchers, open-source communities, technology companies and industrial partners.

Our initial areas of collaboration include:

- Public tender discovery and verification
- AI-powered manufacturer and buyer matching
- Multilingual document analysis
- International trade intelligence
- Supplier qualification and compliance research
- Transparent and responsible AI workflows

Our Principles

Verification before recommendation. Human oversight before action. Transparency before commercial engagement.

We do not claim partnerships with any AI provider or organization unless formally established.

Get Involved

Developers and researchers are invited to open a GitHub Issue to introduce their ideas, propose technical contributions or discuss potential collaboration.

We particularly welcome contributions involving trustworthy data sources, multilingual AI, procurement analysis and responsible automation.

Project Structure

This public repository is dedicated to open collaboration, community proposals and publicly shareable project resources.

Private commercial information, confidential partner data and internal development systems are maintained separately.
